Do I need trading experience?

No prior experience is needed to explore our beginner-focused educational topics. Start with the overview, then ask the team which resources fit your current understanding.

Can I learn before using real money?

Yes. You can begin by learning terms, understanding charts and exploring risk-management concepts. Watching the introduction does not require you to open or fund a trading account.

Are trading results guaranteed?

No. Trading carries a risk of financial loss, and education does not guarantee profitable results. Sky Team's content is educational guidance, not personalised investment advice.

How do I find out about options and fees?

Message Sky Team on Facebook to ask about current participation options, availability and any applicable fees or terms before deciding. This website does not take payments.

How do I register for community access?

For Sky Team's referral-based community access, use the registration link on this page and enter eePfC6mw in VT Markets' Referrer field. Although the broker labels this field optional, the code is needed for Sky Team referral matching. After completing the broker's required steps, message Sky Team to check your registration and receive community access instructions. This page does not automatically approve accounts or grant membership.

Where can I contact Sky Team?

Use the Facebook or Messenger links on this page. Our WhatsApp contact channel is still under construction; Facebook is the place to reach us for now.
