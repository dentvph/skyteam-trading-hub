The SkyTeam Framework

Structure before execution.

Strong decisions start before the entry. Our learning flow turns a busy chart into four repeatable checkpoints you can practice and review.

⌖

Read the market

Identify direction, key levels, and the conditions that would support—or cancel—your idea.

◎

Define the setup

Wait for confirmation and document the reason for entry before placing a position.

◇

Control the risk

Set the invalidation point and position size first. Protecting capital is part of the trade.

↗

Review the result

Journal what happened, separate process from outcome, and carry the lesson forward.
