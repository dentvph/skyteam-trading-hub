Do I need trading experience?

No prior experience is needed to explore our beginner-focused educational topics. Start with the overview, then ask the team which resources fit your current understanding.

Can I learn before using real money?

Yes. You can begin by learning terms, understanding charts and exploring risk-management concepts. Watching the introduction does not require you to open or fund a trading account.

Are trading results guaranteed?

No. Trading carries a risk of financial loss, and education does not guarantee profitable results. Sky Team's content is educational guidance, not personalised investment advice.

How do I find out about options and fees?

Message Sky Team on Facebook to ask about current participation options, availability and any applicable fees or terms before deciding. This website does not take payments.

How do I register for community access?

Submit a public enquiry or visit our Facebook Page and tap Message. A public enquiry does not require a broker account. Private Member Hub access requires a VT Markets account under SkyTeam, verified UID and admin approval.

Where can I contact Sky Team?

Use the Facebook or Messenger links on this page. Our WhatsApp contact channel is still under construction; Facebook is the place to reach us for now.

Privacy on this page

For private membership review, SkyTeam stores your name, VT Markets registered email and UID. Only the owner and authorized hub manager can review these requests and verify referral eligibility. Your approved UID links to one membership; sign-in codes go to the approved email. We never request broker passwords or broker verification codes.

When you submit the community enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, country and optional city, along with your contact consent, referral code and enquiry source. Sky Team uses this information to respond to your enquiry and help with community access. Records are stored on Sky Team's server and are available through the protected CRM to the owner and authorized team members. We do not collect broker passwords, identity documents or payment details in this form.

Registration is required to continue from the welcome screen to the landing page in this browser. You can watch the full introduction without sharing your details. Social links are available inside the website after registration. The registration popup stays open until registration succeeds; it has no close or skip option for unregistered visitors. This browser remembers a successful registration for 30 days using an expiry timestamp, without storing your contact details. Clearing site data or using another browser or device can show the welcome step again. This is a website preference, not a password-protected account.

Opening the overview popup loads an embedded player from YouTube. Selecting a Reel loads a player from Facebook. These platforms may process information under their own privacy policies. Links to Facebook, YouTube and VT Markets open those platforms. Broker registration and any identity checks are handled on VT Markets' website. The hosting service may process basic request information to deliver this page.

Approved members sign in with a one-time email code. Member lessons are protected on the server. Checklist progress and journal entries are saved to your member account; journals are not displayed in the CRM. Email providers process the address and sign-in message needed to deliver your code. Revoking membership ends access.

To ask about your saved details, request a correction or deletion, or withdraw contact consent, message us through our Facebook page.
