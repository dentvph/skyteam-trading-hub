01Risk-management fundamentals

Before any entry, decide what you're willing to lose — and whether you're willing to lose it at all.

  • Position size sets how much each price move is worth to you. Check your broker's contract size for gold, NASDAQ and US30; they differ.
  • Stop-loss plan: write where the idea is wrong before you enter. A stop is a plan, not a guarantee — prices can gap past it.
  • Capital protection: many traders limit risk per idea to a small share of their account and set a daily stop for themselves.

Practise sizing on a demo account until the maths feels routine.

02Trading psychology
  • Discipline means following the plan you wrote when you were calm.
  • Patience: no setup is also a decision. Standing aside is a valid trade.
  • Notice your state: after a loss, a big win or a long day, decisions get faster and worse. Your review journal helps you spot this.
03Market basics
  • Start with the bigger picture: daily and 4-hour charts show the trend and key areas; smaller timeframes show the detail.
  • Structure: higher highs and higher lows describe an uptrend; lower highs and lower lows a downtrend. A break of that pattern is information, not a signal on its own.
  • News moves markets: jobs, inflation and Fed decisions can move gold and indices quickly. Check Market Watch before your session.
04Beginner trading education
  • Learn the terms: pip/point, spread, leverage, margin, lot size, stop-loss, take-profit.
  • Understand that leverage multiplies both gains and losses.
  • Use a demo account first. Results on demo don't guarantee results with real money, but the habits carry over.
05SkyTeam community orientation

Bring questions to the community and to Coach Jong. Share your process, not just your outcomes. Message us on Facebook to ask what to study next.
